### I. Comprehension

#### A. Choose the best answer

1. Satish's mother handed to her son ____________.
   a. bag of pencil
   b. bag of vegetables
   c. big box of chocolates
   d. cricket ball

2. The train was due at ____________.
   a. one o’ clock
   b. twelve o’ clock
   c. two o’ clock
   d. eleven o’ clock

3. The strange lady gave ______ to Arun.
   a. coffee and vadai
   b. tea and bajji
   c. tea and samosas
   d. black tea and cake

4. Arun was sitting on platform _________ .
   a. no. 7
   b. no. 8
   c. no. 4
   d. no. 3

5. Satish and Arun were ________ year old boys.
   a. 12
   b. 11
   c. 13
   d. 10

| No. | Column A | Column B |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | woman in white | Satish's mother |
| 2 | train | boy of same age |
| 3 | bowler | dressed simply |
| 4 | Satish | waves of steam |
| 5 | spectacles | Arun |
