### I. Section-wise Comprehension

#### Section I (Mani)

##### A. Say True or False

1. Mani's hobby is playing cricket.
2. Hobbies are unique to people.
3. People have hobbies only to pass time.

##### B. Choose the correct answer

1. Mani imagined a world with ________.
   a. music and musicians
   b. building and sculptures
   c. magic and magicians

2. When Mani started writing things, he was unable to ________.
   a. describe them accurately
   b. describe them fluently
   c. write clearly

3. Mani feels writing is like _________.
   a. painting the voice
   b. singing a song
   c. dreaming aloud

#### Section II (Ajay Garg)

##### A. Write True or False

1. Ajay Garg is an artist.
2. Asha Devi taught Ajay the miniature painting.
3. Ajay displayed 150 paintings.
4. Ajay was awarded by the Indian president Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.

##### B. Answer the following questions

1. What was Ajay gifted with?
2. How was he honoured?
3. What is Ajay's current goal?
4. How does Ajay turn his hobby into a successful career?

### II. Read and Understand (Overall)

#### A. Fill in the blanks

1. Mani's grandfather noticed it and helped him by reading __________.
2. Ajay mastered the techniques of _______.
3. Ilavazhagi won her first match against _______.

#### B. Choose the correct synonyms for the italicized word

1. Writing is a *unique* hobby.
   a. common
   b. special / uncommon
   c. simple

2. Ajay started to paint *happily*.
   a. joyfully
   b. sorrowfully
   c. angrily

3. Miniature paintings *depict* Indian culture.
   a. hide
   b. portray / show
   c. damage

#### C. Choose the correct antonyms for the italicized word

1. Ajay *suffered* an injury.
   a. endured
   b. enjoyed
   c. felt

2. Ajay *mastered* the techniques of painting.
   a. learned
   b. failed / unlearned
   c. skilled

3. Ilavazhagi *won* the world championship in 2008.
   a. lost
   b. succeeded
   c. gained

#### D. Answer the following questions in one or two sentences

1. What happened to Ajay when he was three?
2. What did Asha Devi teach Ajay?
3. Why is writing beneficial according to Mani?

#### E. Answer the following questions in about 100 words

1. How did Ajay’s father find his son’s talent?
2. Write about Ilavazhagi and her achievements.

### IV. Grammar (Degrees of Comparison)

#### a. Answer the questions comparing the two items

1. Which is faster? A train or a plane?
2. Which is cheaper? Gold or Silver?
3. Which is larger? City or village?
4. Which is bigger? A sea or an ocean?
5. Which is taller? A giraffe or a camel?

#### b. Complete the sentences with positive, comparative, and superlative degrees

1. Town – city – village (*quiet*)
2. Istanbul – Moscow – London (*populated*)
3. Windy weather – warm weather – rainy weather (*good*)
4. Ocean – river – lake (*deep*)
5. The USA – Russia – Spain (*large*)
