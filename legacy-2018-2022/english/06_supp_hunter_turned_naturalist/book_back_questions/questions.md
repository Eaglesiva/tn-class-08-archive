### I. Comprehension

#### A. Fill in the blanks

1. Jim Corbett died on ____________.
2. Corbett shot wild animals in his ____________.
3. _______________ was the first man-eater shot by Corbett.
4. Corbett shot the tigress dead, near the _________.

#### B. Read the following passage and answer the questions given below

> *"Jim Corbett played a key role in establishing India's first national park in the Kumaon Hills, the Hailey National Park in Uttarakhand, India. It was initially named after Lord Malcolm Hailey. Jim Corbett died on 19 April 1955. The park was renamed in 1957 as The Jim Corbett National Park to honor his conservation efforts."*

1. Who played a key role in establishing the national park?
2. Why was the park named Hailey National Park?
3. When was it renamed as Jim Corbett National Park?

#### C. Answer the following in one or two words

1. What was the birth name of Jim Corbett?
2. What was the name of Corbett’s dog?

#### D. Answer the following in about 100 words

1. According to Corbett, why do tigers turn into man-eaters?
2. Describe Corbett’s efforts in tracking down the Champawat tigress.
