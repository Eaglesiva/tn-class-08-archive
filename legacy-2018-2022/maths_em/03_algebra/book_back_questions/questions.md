## Recap (book pp. 74-75)

Answer the following questions:

1. Write the number of terms in the following expressions
   - (i) $x + y + z - xyz$
   - (ii) $m^2n^2c$
   - (iii) $a^2b^2c - ab^2c^2 + a^2bc^2 + 3abc$
   - (iv) $8x^2 - 4xy + 7xy^2$

2. Identify the numerical co-efficient of each term in the following expressions.
   - (i) $2x^2 - 5xy + 6y^2 + 7x - 10y + 9$
   - (ii) $\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{2y}{5} - xy + 7$

3. Pick out the like terms from the following: $x^2,\ 3y,\ 3a^2b^2,\ 4x,\ x^2y,\ 9p^2,\ -3y,\ 4ba^2,\ 9ab,\ 7q,\ 8p,\ -25x,\ -5x^2,\ 2x,\ 9x^2y,\ -9p^2,\ qp^2,\ 2xy^2,\ -10p^2,\ q,\ 3y^2x,\ \dfrac{2}{3}p,\ -x^2,\ a^2b^2,\ -ab,\ a^2b,\ 2ba,\ m^3n^2,\ 5m^3n^2$

   [Picture: a bowl containing the above terms and five glasses, indicative illustration only. A "Like Terms" note box beside it explains that the variables of the terms along with their respective exponents must be the same, with examples $x^2, 4x^2$; $a^2b^2, -5a^2b^2$; $2m, -7m$.]

4. Add : $2x, 6y, 9x, -2y$

5. Simplify : $(5x^3y^3 - 3x^2y^2 + xy + 7) + (2xy + x^3y^3 - 5 + 2x^2y^2)$

6. The sides of a triangle are $2x - 5y + 9$, $3y + 6x - 7$ and $-4x + y + 10$. Find the perimeter of the triangle.

7. Subtract $-2mn$ from $6mn$.

8. Subtract $6a^2 - 5ab + 3b^2$ from $4a^2 - 3ab + b^2$.

9. The length of a log is $3a + 4b - 2$ and a piece $(2a - b)$ is removed from it. What is the length of the remaining log? [Picture: a log of wood with the piece $(2a-b)$ marked as removed, indicative only.]

10. A tin had $x$ litres of oil. Another tin had $(3x^2 + 6x - 5)$ litres of oil. The shopkeeper added $(x+7)$ litres more to the second tin. Later, he sold $(x^2+6)$ litres of oil from the second tin. How much oil was left in the second tin?
