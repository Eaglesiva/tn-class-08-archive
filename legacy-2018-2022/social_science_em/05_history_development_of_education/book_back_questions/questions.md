**Match-the-following verification (Section III)**
The printed pairs were checked one by one against the chapter text:
- University Grants Commission with 1964: contradicted. The Page 53 print image says the University Grants Commission "was set up in 1956". The printed 1964 is the date of the Kothari Commission on the same page.
- Kothari Commission with English Education: contradicted. The Page 53 print image dates the Kothari Commission to 1964 and lists its recommendations. English Education is the subject of Macaulay's Minute (Page 50 print image).
- Macaulay with Bengaluru: contradicted. The Page 50 print image says Macaulay prepared his Minute of 1835 that made English the medium of education. Bengaluru is the location of the Indian Institute of Science on the Page 53 print image.
- Free and compulsory education with 1956: contradicted. The Page 53 print image lists "Free and compulsory education for children aged 6-14 years" as a Kothari Commission recommendation.
- Tata's Indian Institute of Science with 6-14 years: contradicted. The Page 53 print image says "Jamsetji Tata's Indian Institute of Science (1909), Bengaluru".
- Conclusion: all five printed pairings are contradicted by the chapter's own facts. The printed right-hand values are shuffled, so the table is not presented as a Markdown table. Each column is listed separately, in printed order, with no pairing asserted.
