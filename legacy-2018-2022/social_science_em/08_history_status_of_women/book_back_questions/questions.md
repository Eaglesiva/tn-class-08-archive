**Match-the-following verification (Section III)**
The printed pairs were checked one by one against the chapter text:
- Theosophical society with Italian traveler: contradicted. The Page 80 print image says "Theosophical society was established at Chennai and Dr. Annie Besant who came from Europe and joined it". The Italian traveler is Niccolo Conti (Page 78 print image).
- Sarada Sadan with Social evil: contradicted. The Page 80 print image says "In 1889, Pandita Ramabai opened Sarada Sadan (Home of Learning) for Hindu widows in Bombay". Social evil is not a description of the institution.
- Wood's Despatch with Annie Besant: contradicted. The Page 77 print image says "Charles Wood's despatch on education in 1854". Annie Besant is linked to the Theosophical society on the Page 80 print image.
- Niccolo Conti with Pandita RamaBhai: contradicted. The Page 78 print image says "The Italian traveler, Niccolo Conti, who visited Vijayanagar about the year A.D. (C.E) 1420". Pandita Ramabai is linked to Sarada Sadan on the Page 80 print image.
- Dowry with 1854: contradicted. The Page 78 print image lists the "dowry system" among the factors behind female infanticide. The date 1854 belongs to Wood's Despatch on the Page 77 print image.
- Conclusion: all five printed pairings are contradicted by the chapter's own facts. The table is therefore listed as two unpaired columns, each in printed order. The chapter facts that the table should have reflected are Theosophical society with Annie Besant, Sarada Sadan with Pandita Ramabai, Wood's Despatch with 1854, Niccolo Conti with Italian traveler and Dowry with Social evil.
