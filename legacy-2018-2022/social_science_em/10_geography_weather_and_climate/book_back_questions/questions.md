## Extraction Flags

- **Excluded non-evaluation content:** the Glossary table; the Reference Books list; the Internet Resources list; the ICT Corner box on the Page 103 print image; all QR codes.
- **Match-the-following verification:** each pairing was checked against the chapter's own text before choosing the table format. All five printed pairings are contradicted by the chapter, so the table is reproduced as two separate lists.
  - Climate to Locating and Tracking Storms: contradicted. The Page 96 print image defines climate as "the average conditions (state) of the weather of a place or a region" measured over a long period (about 35 years).
  - Isonif to Cyclone: contradicted. "Isonif" is not defined in the chapter; the Page 97 print image lists the isolines (Isotherm, Isocryme, Isohel, Isollobar, Isobar). The Page 99 print image defines cyclones as low-pressure systems, so "Cyclone" is not the item that pairs with an isoline.
  - Hygrometer to Equal Snowfall: contradicted. The Page 100 print image says "Hygrometer is used to measure the humidity."
  - Radar to Long Term Changes: contradicted. Radar is not mentioned in the chapter text, and the Page 96 print image ties the long-term measurement of weather elements to climate, not to radar.
  - Low Pressure to Humidity: contradicted. The Page 99 print image says low pressure "leads to cloudiness, wind, and precipitation", not to humidity.
- **Factual oddities kept verbatim:** "Which one the following represents places with equal amount of rainfall" (Question I.4 is missing "of"; kept as printed). "Isonif" in the match table is not a term defined in the chapter; kept as printed. "Humidity is calculated by Aneroid Barometer" (True or False IV.4); the Page 100 print image says a hygrometer measures humidity, so the statement contradicts the chapter; kept as printed. "The Scientific study of weather is called Climatology" (True or False IV.2) and "The scientific study of weather is called [printed blank]" (Fill in the blanks II.2): the Page 96 print image defines the scientific study of climate as climatology, not weather; both kept as printed. "Isohel refers equal sunshine" (True or False IV.3) is consistent with the Page 97 print image and is kept as printed.

- **Spacing normalisation (not text change):** Question I.4 options are printed without a space after the letter (for example "a)isotherm") and are reproduced as "a) isotherm". The Activities table in item X.3 is blank in print and is reproduced as an empty table with the same row labels.
