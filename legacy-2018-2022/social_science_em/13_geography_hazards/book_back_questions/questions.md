## I. Choose the correct answer

   a) 78.09%
   b) 74.08%
   c) 80.07%
   d) 76.63%
2. Tsunami in Indian Ocean took place in the year _______.
   a) 1990
   b) 2004
   c) 2005
   d) 2008
3. The word Tsunami is derived from _______ language.
   a) Hindi
   b) French
   c) Japanese
   d) German
4. The example of surface water is
   a) Artesian well
   b) Groundwater
   c) Subsurface water
   d) Lake
5. Event that occurs due to the failure of monsoons.
   a) Condensation
   b) Drought
   c) Evaporation
   d) Precipitation
